Our Story

A Company Built On Trust

Ponvasantham Construction Private Limited was founded in 2011 by P. Murugan in Pondicherry, starting with small residential projects built on a simple promise: quality work, done honestly, and supervised from start to finish.

Over the past 15 years, that approach has taken the company across Tamil Nadu, with 35+ completed projects spanning residential homes, commercial buildings, renovations, landscaping, swimming pools, and driveways. Every site is still personally supervised, the same way it was on day one.
